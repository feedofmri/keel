The guarantee

A bound you can write into policy

Pick α. Keel's error stays under it. Naive rules don't.

Per bug · conformal risk control

(Σ wrong·1[s ≤ λ] + 1) / (n + 1) ≤ α

At most α of bugs get a wrong merge, on average.

Per merge · Learn-then-Test

P(Bin(k, α) ≤ w) ≤ δ / |Λ|

At most α of merges are wrong, with probability 1 − δ.

Assumes new bugs resemble the calibration bugs. Without calibration, Keel never auto-merges. Details
